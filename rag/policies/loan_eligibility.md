# Loan Eligibility and Conditions

**Document ID:** POL-LOAN-001
**Category:** Loans
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Status:** Active

> DISCLAIMER: This is a fictional policy document created for demonstration
> purposes only. It does not constitute actual banking policy, legal advice,
> or regulatory guidance.

## 1. Personal Loan Eligibility

Applicants must satisfy all of the following criteria:

- Minimum age: 21 years at the time of application
- Maximum age: 60 years at loan maturity
- Minimum annual income: INR 300,000
- Minimum employment tenure: 12 months with current employer (salaried) or
  24 months of business operation (self-employed)
- Credit score: minimum 650 from an approved credit bureau

## 2. Loan Amount and Tenure

- Minimum loan amount: INR 50,000
- Maximum loan amount: INR 2,500,000 (subject to income multiples)
- Loan tenure: 12 to 60 months
- Maximum EMI-to-income ratio: 50% (including all existing obligations)

## 3. Interest Rate and Fees

- Interest rate: 10.5% to 18.0% per annum, determined by credit profile
- Processing fee: 1.0% to 2.5% of sanctioned amount (non-refundable)
- Prepayment charge: 2% of outstanding principal if repaid within 12 months;
  no charge thereafter
- Late payment fee: INR 500 plus 2% per month on overdue EMI amount

## 4. Required Documentation

- Identity and address proof (as per KYC policy POL-KYC-001)
- Income proof: last 3 months' salary slips and latest Form 16 (salaried) or
  last 2 years' ITR and audited financials (self-employed)
- Bank statements for the last 6 months
- Existing loan sanction letters (if applicable)

## 5. Disbursement Conditions

- Loan is disbursed only after completion of KYC and credit assessment
- Funds are credited to the applicant's BankSphere savings or current account
- Standing instruction for EMI deduction must be registered before disbursement
- First EMI falls due 30 days after disbursement

## 6. Default and Recovery

An account is classified as a non-performing asset (NPA) if EMI payments are
overdue by more than 90 days. Recovery proceedings follow the bank's
established recovery and collections policy.
