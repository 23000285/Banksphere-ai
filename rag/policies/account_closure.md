# Account Closure and Dormancy

## 1. Voluntary Account Closure

A customer may request account closure by submitting a written application
at the home branch or through the secure online banking portal. The following
conditions must be met:

- All linked loans and credit facilities must be fully repaid
- All issued cheques must have cleared or been cancelled
- All standing instructions and auto-debit mandates must be revoked
- The debit and credit cards linked to the account must be surrendered
- Minimum zero balance with no pending charges

## 2. Closure Charges

- Closure within 14 days of opening: no charge (cooling-off period)
- Closure within 12 months of opening: INR 500 processing fee
- Closure after 12 months: no charge

## 3. Dormant Account Classification

An account is classified as dormant if there are no customer-initiated
transactions (credit or debit) for a continuous period of 24 months.
Interest credits, bank charges, and system-generated entries do not count
as customer-initiated transactions.

## 4. Reactivation of Dormant Accounts

To reactivate a dormant account, the customer must:

- Visit the home branch in person with valid identity proof
- Submit a reactivation request form
- Complete updated KYC verification if existing KYC has expired
- Perform a minimum initial transaction

Dormant accounts cannot be reactivated through internet or mobile banking.

## 5. Unclaimed Deposits

Balances in accounts that remain dormant for 10 years or more are
transferred to the Depositor Education and Awareness Fund as per
regulatory requirements. Customers may claim such balances by applying
to the bank with appropriate identity proof and documentation.
