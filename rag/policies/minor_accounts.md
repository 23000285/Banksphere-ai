# Minor Accounts and Guardian Requirements

**Document ID:** POL-MINOR-001
**Category:** Minor Accounts
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Status:** Active

> DISCLAIMER: This is a fictional policy document created for demonstration
> purposes only. It does not constitute actual banking policy, legal advice,
> or regulatory guidance.

## 1. Account Opening for Minors

A savings account may be opened for a minor (under 18 years of age) by a
natural guardian (parent) or a court-appointed legal guardian. The guardian
must complete full KYC verification for both themselves and the minor.

## 2. Required Documents

- Birth certificate or school identity card of the minor
- Guardian's government-issued photo ID and address proof
- Proof of relationship (birth certificate, court order, or legal guardianship document)

## 3. Operational Restrictions

Accounts opened for minors are subject to the following restrictions:

- No overdraft or credit facilities
- No cheque book issued for minors below 12 years
- Daily transaction limit of INR 10,000 for ATM withdrawals
- Internet and mobile banking restricted to view-only for minors below 15 years
- All debit transactions above INR 25,000 require guardian co-authorization

## 4. Sole Operation by Minor

A minor who has attained 10 years of age may be permitted to operate a savings
account independently, provided:

- The guardian gives written consent
- The account balance does not exceed INR 100,000
- No loan or credit product is linked to the account

## 5. Conversion to Regular Account

On attaining 18 years of age, the minor account must be converted to a
regular savings account. The customer must complete fresh KYC verification
in their own name within 30 days of turning 18. Failure to do so will result
in the account being frozen until KYC is completed.
