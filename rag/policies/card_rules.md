# Card Rules, Blocking, and Replacement

**Document ID:** POL-CARD-001
**Category:** Cards
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Status:** Active

> DISCLAIMER: This is a fictional policy document created for demonstration
> purposes only. It does not constitute actual banking policy, legal advice,
> or regulatory guidance.

## 1. Card Issuance

Debit cards are issued automatically with every new savings or current account.
Credit cards are issued upon separate application and credit assessment.

- Debit card activation: via ATM PIN set or mobile banking activation
- Credit card activation: via IVR call, SMS confirmation, or mobile app
- Virtual cards may be generated instantly through the mobile application

## 2. Transaction Limits

Default daily transaction limits are as follows:

| Card Type | ATM Withdrawal | POS / Online | Contactless |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic Debit | INR 25,000 | INR 100,000 | INR 5,000 per tap |
| Platinum Debit | INR 50,000 | INR 200,000 | INR 10,000 per tap |
| Credit Card | N/A | As per credit limit | INR 5,000 per tap |

Customers may request temporary limit increases through the mobile app or
by contacting the branch. Temporary increases are valid for a maximum of
7 calendar days.

## 3. Card Blocking

A card may be blocked immediately by the customer through any of the
following channels:

- Mobile banking app (instant self-service block)
- 24/7 helpline (IVR or agent-assisted)
- Branch visit with valid identity proof
- SMS keyword BLOCK sent to the registered short code

Once a card is blocked, it cannot be unblocked. A replacement card must be
requested.

## 4. Card Replacement

Replacement cards are issued within 7 to 10 working days for standard
delivery, or within 3 working days for express delivery (additional fee
of INR 500 applies). The replacement card will have a new number and CVV.

## 5. International Usage

International transactions are disabled by default and must be enabled by
the customer through the mobile app or branch. International enablement
is valid for a maximum of 30 days per activation and automatically reverts
to domestic-only mode.

## 6. Lost or Stolen Cards

Customers must report lost or stolen cards immediately. The bank is not
liable for unauthorized transactions occurring before the card is reported
as lost. Refer to the fraud disputes policy (POL-FRAUD-001) for the
liability framework.
