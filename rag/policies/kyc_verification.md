## 1. Identity Verification Requirements

All new customers must complete Know Your Customer (KYC) verification before
account activation. The following documents are accepted as valid proof of
identity:

- Government-issued photo ID (passport, driving licence, or national ID card)
- Permanent Account Number (PAN) card
- Aadhaar card or Aadhaar enrolment slip with photograph

At least two independent identity documents must be submitted. Photocopies
must be attested by the branch manager or an authorized bank officer.

## 2. Address Verification

Proof of address must be no older than three months and may include:

- Utility bill (electricity, water, gas, or telephone)
- Bank statement from another financial institution
- Registered rental agreement or property deed
- Government-issued address proof (voter ID, Aadhaar)

## 3. KYC Renewal and Periodic Review

KYC records must be renewed at the following intervals:

- High-risk customers: every 12 months
- Medium-risk customers: every 24 months
- Low-risk customers: every 60 months

Failure to complete KYC renewal may result in account restrictions, including
suspension of outgoing transactions until documentation is updated.

## 4. Enhanced Due Diligence

Enhanced due diligence applies to politically exposed persons (PEPs),
customers from high-risk jurisdictions, and accounts with unusual transaction
patterns. Additional documentation, source-of-funds declarations, and senior
management approval are required.

## 5. Digital KYC (e-KYC)

Video-based KYC is permitted for remote account opening, subject to:

- Live video interaction with a trained bank representative
- Real-time capture of identity document and face match
- Geo-tagging and timestamp of the video session
- Secure storage of the recorded session for audit purposes
