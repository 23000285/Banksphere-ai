# Fraud Disputes and Suspicious Transactions

**Document ID:** POL-FRAUD-001
**Category:** Fraud
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01
**Status:** Active

> DISCLAIMER: This is a fictional policy document created for demonstration
> purposes only. It does not constitute actual banking policy, legal advice,
> or regulatory guidance.

## 1. Reporting a Fraudulent Transaction

Customers must report unauthorized or suspicious transactions within 72 hours
of occurrence by calling the 24/7 helpline, visiting a branch, or using the
in-app dispute form.

## 2. Provisional Credit

Upon receiving a valid dispute, the bank will issue a provisional credit of the
disputed amount within 10 working days, provided:

- The customer files a written or digital complaint
- A copy of the relevant transaction record is attached
- The customer has not shared their PIN, OTP, or credentials voluntarily

## 3. Investigation Timeline

- The bank must complete its investigation within 30 calendar days of the
  complaint for domestic transactions
- For international or cross-border transactions, the investigation period
  extends to 60 calendar days
- The customer will be informed of the outcome in writing or via registered
  email

## 4. Liability Framework

Customer liability for unauthorized electronic transactions depends on the
reporting timeline:

| Reporting Window | Customer Liability |
|---|---|
| Within 3 working days | Zero liability |
| Between 4 and 7 working days | Maximum INR 10,000 |
| Beyond 7 working days | As determined by bank policy, up to full amount |

## 5. Suspicious Transaction Monitoring

The bank maintains automated transaction monitoring systems that flag
transactions based on:

- Deviations from established spending patterns
- Transactions from high-risk geographies
- Rapid successive transactions within a short time window
- Transactions exceeding velocity thresholds per channel

Flagged transactions may be temporarily held pending customer verification.
The customer will be notified via SMS and email when a transaction is held.

## 6. Account Freeze

The bank reserves the right to freeze an account suspected of being involved
in fraud, money laundering, or other illegal activity. The customer will be
notified within 24 hours and provided instructions for resolution.
